Attendance Policy

Attendance confirmation is collected separately from slot booking to avoid advance no-shows. Confirmed players gain eligibility to reserve court spots during the 7-day window.

Cancel slots before the T-48h deadline without penalty. Vacated spots are immediately allocated to waitlisted players in sequential First-In-First-Out (FIFO) queue order.
