Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about permanent accounts, the 15-day cadence, 30-minute court slots, and the 2-slot quota.

You only register your personal profile once to receive a permanent Unique Player ID (e.g. MP1001 for male, FP1002 for female).

Instead of forcing participants to commit to an entire year of dates months in advance, we run one event approximately every 15 days. Players confirm attendance and pick specific 30-minute slots closer to each date when their personal schedules are known.
To ensure fair access across our community, each player can hold a maximum of two 30-minute slots per event evening (representing 60 minutes of total court time).

These can be two consecutive intervals on the same court (e.g., 7:00 PM – 7:30 PM followed by 7:30 PM – 8:00 PM), or spaced intervals. The platform automatically blocks any player from reserving a 3rd slot or booking overlapping times.
With 10 courts available over a 3-hour evening (7:00 PM to 10:00 PM), each court offers six 30-minute intervals:

10 courts × 6 slots per court = 60 court-slot units

In doubles sports like Pickleball and Paddle Ball, 4 players share a court at any given time. This yields a total capacity of up to 240 player appearances across the evening. As stated in our framework, 240 is not the number of separate time slots, but the maximum player appearances under a 4-player shared format.
Every 15-day event follows a strict, predictable window:
  • 7 Days Before (T-7d at 09:00 AM): Slot booking opens. All registered players can log in, confirm attendance, and select up to 2 slots.
  • 48 Hours Before (T-48h at 07:00 PM): Booking closes and attendance locks. Cancellations freeze to allow organizers to build match fixtures and assign referees.
  • Day Before / Event Day: Final schedules and digital match passes are published to players.
When hundreds of community players attempt to reserve popular slots at 09:00 AM, our backend executes pessimistic row-level locks (PostgreSQL SELECT ... FOR UPDATE) within atomic database transactions.

Concurrent transactions for the same court-slot are queued sequentially, and kernel-level CHECK constraints prohibit booked_count > max_capacity. If 20 players click the last spot at the same millisecond, exactly 1 succeeds and 19 are safely redirected to the waitlist.
Default passwords present serious security vulnerabilities where accounts can be hijacked or altered by third parties.

Under our approved security design, when a player registers, an account setup token is generated and emailed. The player sets their own private password, which is hashed with Argon2id.
If a slot reaches full capacity (e.g. 4/4 in Pickleball), players can join a First-In-First-Out (FIFO) waitlist. If any confirmed player cancels before the 48-hour deadline, position #1 on the waitlist is automatically promoted to confirmed and notified immediately via SMS/Email.
